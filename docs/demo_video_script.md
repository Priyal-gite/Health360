# End-to-End Demonstration Video Script

**Target length:** 8–12 minutes. Record only synthetic or approved de-identified information. Hide credentials, connection strings, direct identifiers, and restricted records. Add captions or a transcript.

1. **Purpose and limits (0:00–0:45):** Explain the population-level questions and that the project is not a diagnostic tool.
2. **Source and approval (0:45–1:30):** Identify the approved data source, coverage period, access approval, and extraction approach. If the source has not been approved, demonstrate only with clearly labeled synthetic data.
3. **Preparation and quality (1:30–2:45):** Show field mapping, exclusions, missingness, invalid-value handling, duplicate counts, data dictionary, and row-count reconciliation.
4. **Tableau connection (2:45–3:30):** Show the approved connection or governed extract, refresh mode, and calculation inventory without exposing credentials.
5. **Visual analysis (3:30–5:30):** Walk through KPI cards, age and BMI comparisons, clinical/lifestyle/symptom views, heatmap denominators, and privacy-reviewed cohort view.
6. **Dashboard and story (5:30–7:00):** Demonstrate coordinated filters, reset, responsive layouts, and all supported story scenes.
7. **Performance (7:00–8:00):** Report measured dataset volume, load/filter timings, calculation count, visualization count, test setup, and unresolved issues. Do not invent results.
8. **Flask integration and release (8:00–9:30):** Show the embedded dashboard and story, access behavior, loading/error state, and approved published location.
9. **Documentation and next steps (9:30–10:00):** Point to the setup/runbook, data dictionary, performance results, known limitations, and maintenance steps.
